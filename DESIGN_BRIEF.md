# Logo brief: Delta — "Know the difference"

## 1. What I need
A logo for a student machine-learning project called **Delta**, tagline **"Know the difference."**
Please design: a symbol (mark), a wordmark, a horizontal lockup (mark + "Delta"), a lockup with the tagline, and a favicon version that still reads at 16 × 16 px.

## 2. What the product is
Delta is a website that tells you **what a trip will really cost** and **how much a vehicle's real-road mileage differs from the number on its brochure**.

You pick a vehicle (bike, scooter, car or electric scooter), say how you ride (city, mixed or highway, load, age, AC on or off), type today's fuel price and the trip distance. Twelve machine-learning models predict the mileage and the site turns it into rupees. It also shows what the trip costs each month and each year.

It is built with Python, Flask and scikit-learn. It runs on the user's own computer. It is for an Indian audience: prices are in rupees, vehicles are Indian bikes and scooters, cars and electric scooters.

## 3. Why the name "Delta" (Δ = difference)
Every important screen in the product is about a difference. Use these as the idea behind the logo:

| Difference | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| Rated mileage vs real-road mileage | e.g. 62.8 km/l on the brochure vs 52.4 km/l on your roads (×0.83) |
| One model vs another | six models predict a trip at ₹39.91 to ₹50.87, so the spread is the uncertainty |
| Training score vs test score | R² 0.985 on training rows vs 0.849 on new rows shows overfitting |
| City vs highway | the same trip costs more in city traffic |
| One vehicle vs another | which bike or car costs less to run |
| Petrol vs diesel vs CNG vs electric | cost per kilometre |

Tagline meaning: the product helps people stop trusting the headline number and see the gap.

## 4. Audience and tone
- People who commute daily or are buying a bike, scooter, car or electric scooter in India; also a college evaluation panel.
- Tone: calm, clear and trustworthy. Friendly, not corporate. Smart but not technical-intimidating.
- It should feel like a precise instrument (gauge, odometer) made soft and approachable.

## 5. Visual identity already in use (please match)
- **Palette (Sage Garden):** sage `#7c9082`, deep green ink `#355043`, warm off-white `#f8f7f4`, near-black text `#1a1f2e`, soft sand `#e0c47f`, soft coral `#de9d92`, mint `#8cba9a`.
- **Dark mode:** charcoal `#0c0f0d` background, sage and light green `#bcd8c3`.
- **Type:** headings in *Bricolage Grotesque* (bold, slightly quirky), body in *Figtree*.
- **Shape language:** large rounded corners, soft shadows, a semicircular gauge with three soft bands (coral = thirsty, sand = balanced, mint = efficient) and a needle, and a rolling odometer with white digit tiles.
- Current placeholder logo: a small milestone-stone shape with a sage cap. Delta replaces it.

## 6. Logo ideas to explore (please try these, and add your own)
1. **Triangle as a gauge needle.** A clean Δ whose right edge is a needle sweeping across a short arc. One side is the "rated" number, the other the "real" number.
2. **Two overlapping triangles.** One solid sage, one outline. The sliver where they do not match is the difference. Reads as "the gap".
3. **Δ built from a road.** A triangle formed by two lanes converging, with a small marker at the point.
4. **Δ with an odometer tile.** The triangle sits inside or beside a rounded white digit tile, echoing the product's odometer.
Prefer the simplest idea that still looks distinctive. One idea, executed cleanly.

## 7. Requirements
- Works in one colour (sage, black and white versions) and on light and dark backgrounds.
- Mark must stay recognisable at 16 px (favicon) and at 22 px tall (website header).
- Geometric, with few anchor points, so it also works as a simple SVG.
- Wordmark: the word "Delta" in or close to Bricolage Grotesque, with consideration for the Δ shape (for example the "A" or the "D" echoing the triangle). Tagline "Know the difference." in Figtree, sentence case.
- Enough clear space and a minimum size note.

## 8. Please avoid
- Fuel drops, gas pumps, speedometers drawn as a cliché, steering wheels, cars or motorbike silhouettes.
- Neon or bright gradients, glossy 3D effects, and heavy outlines.
- Purple or blue "AI" look. Stay in the sage, sand and coral family.
- Putting the word "difference" in the mark itself. It belongs in the tagline only.

## 9. Deliverables
- SVG for: mark, wordmark, horizontal lockup, lockup with tagline.
- Colour (sage on off-white), one-colour black, one-colour white, and a dark-mode version.
- A 32 × 32 and a 16 × 16 favicon preview.
- A short note on the idea and the chosen colours.

## 10. Where it will be used
Website header (about 22 px tall) and browser-tab icon, README and the project report cover, presentation title slide, and a possible sticker or poster for the demo.
