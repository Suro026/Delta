How a prediction works

Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  1. Choose your vehicle

    Pick a brand and model from the real datasets and its engine specs fill in. Or type your own numbers.

  2. Models predict mileage

    Linear, Ridge, Decision Tree, KNN, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting each predict. A second set of six classifiers rates it Thirsty, Balanced or Efficient.

  3. You enter today's price

    Type the price at your pump. Cost is distance ÷ real-road mileage × that price.
